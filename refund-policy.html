Shipping, Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Standard Customer Protection Policy

1. Nationwide Shipping & Delivery

  • Delivery Coverage: We provide delivery services across all regions in Ghana, dispatched directly from our store at Kejetia Market, Kumasi.
  • Transparent Delivery Fees: Delivery fees are calculated dynamically at checkout based on distance and estimated travel time, and are shown clearly before you make payment. There are no hidden fees.
  • Delivery Timelines:
    • Greater Kumasi: Same-day delivery or within 24 hours via reliable local dispatch riders.
    • Other Regions in Ghana (Accra, Tema, Takoradi, Sunyani, Cape Coast, Koforidua, Tamale, etc.): 1 to 2 business days via intercity bus parcel services (VIP, STC, OA).
  • Free Store Pickup: You can always choose free pickup at our shop in Kejetia Market. Once you receive your "Ready for Pickup" notification, you can collect your order during our business hours (Mon–Sat, 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Sunday closed).
  • Accurate Location & Phone: Please ensure your phone number is active and reachable. The courier will call you when approaching your delivery point. If the rider cannot reach you after multiple attempts, re-delivery may attract an additional courier fee.

2. Returns & Exchanges

  • You can request an exchange or return within 3 calendar days of receiving your order (from date of delivery or store pickup).
  • Condition Requirement: Returned clothes must be strictly unworn, unwashed, unaltered, free of perfumes or stains, and in original packaging with all product tags attached.
  • Size or Item Exchange: If an item does not fit, we will happily exchange it for another size or an alternative style of equal value in our shop, subject to stock availability.
  • Initiating an Exchange: Contact us with your Order Number (e.g. KD-1025) via WhatsApp, phone, or website live chat to arrange the return.

3. Refund Processing (Via Paystack)

  • Once the returned items are received and inspected at our Kejetia shop, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.
  • Payment Method: Approved refunds are reversed directly back to the same account you paid with (Mobile Money or Bank Card) via our payment gateway, Paystack.
  • Refund Timeline: We initiate the refund within 3 working days of approval. Paystack typically credits your Mobile Money wallet or bank card within 3 to 5 business days depending on your network provider (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo) or issuing bank.
  • Order Cancellations: If you cancel an order before it has been dispatched from our store, you will receive a full 100% refund, including delivery fees.

4. Wrong, Damaged or Defective Items

  • Every piece of clothing is carefully inspected before packaging. In the unlikely event that you receive the wrong size, wrong color, or a defective item, please notify us within 48 hours with a photo of the item.
  • We will promptly replace the item free of charge or issue a full refund, and KD Wisdom Enterprise will cover all courier costs involved.

5. Non-Returnable Items

  • Items that have been worn, washed, stained, altered, or damaged after delivery.
  • Underwear and socks, due to strict hygiene and health standards (unless a manufacturer defect is reported upon opening).
  • Items explicitly marked as "Clearance" or "Final Sale".

6. Wholesale & Bulk Orders

  • Wholesale orders (orders of 3+ pieces at bulk pricing) are eligible for exchange or refund only for damaged, defective, or incorrect pieces reported within 48 hours of delivery or collection.
  • Wholesale buyers are kindly advised to count and verify all packages upon arrival.
