Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Data Protection & Customer Privacy Guidelines

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

KD Wisdom Enterprise ("we", "our", or "the store"), located at Kejetia Market, Kumasi, Ashanti Region, Ghana, is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal data of our customers, website visitors, and wholesale partners.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit kdwisdomenterprise.com, create an account, communicate with us, or place an order, and how we safeguard and utilize that data in accordance with Ghanaian data protection principles.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide an excellent shopping and delivery experience:

  • Account Information: Your full name, email address, and phone number when you register an account or sign in with Google.
  • Order & Delivery Details: Your delivery address, landmark descriptions, GPS coordinates or map locations selected at checkout, recipient contact numbers, and clothing size/color selections.
  • Communications: Messages sent through our website live chat, email inquiries, or WhatsApp messages regarding sizing, order status, or wholesale inquiries.
  • Technical Data: Standard browser information, IP address, device type, and shopping bag session cookies to maintain your shopping cart.

3. Payment Security & Financial Information (Paystack)

  • All payments on our website are processed through Paystack, an accredited, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment service provider.
  • We do NOT store or have access to your Mobile Money PIN, banking passwords, or card CVV/security codes.
  • When making a Mobile Money payment (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money) or paying with a Visa/Mastercard card, all sensitive transaction data is encrypted directly via Paystack's secure payment infrastructure.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, package, and fulfill your retail or wholesale clothing orders.
  • To communicate order confirmation receipts, SMS updates, and delivery dispatch notices.
  • To provide dispatch riders and bus terminal couriers with the necessary landmark details and phone numbers to locate you quickly.
  • To respond to your live chat questions regarding sizes, fits, stock, and exchanges.
  • To prevent fraudulent orders and maintain platform security.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted operational partners strictly for fulfilling your order:

  • Delivery Couriers & Parcel Services: Dispatch riders in Kumasi and intercity bus parcel services (VIP, STC) receive only the recipient's name, phone number, and delivery destination.
  • Payment Gateways: Paystack receives transaction totals and customer identifiers to authorize and complete transactions.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Secure databases (Supabase) host encrypted customer records and authentication tokens.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses local browser storage and minimal functional cookies solely to:

  • Preserve items in your shopping bag between page visits.
  • Remember your logged-in session so you do not have to sign in repeatedly.
  • Store pre-set checkpoint preferences in the administrative portal.

7. Data Retention & Customer Rights

  • You have the right to inspect, update, or correct your personal information at any time through your Account Dashboard.
  • You may request the permanent deletion of your account, message history, or order logs by reaching out to us via live chat or email.
  • We retain transactional order data only as long as required for accounting, tax verification, and warranty/return obligations under Ghanaian law.
