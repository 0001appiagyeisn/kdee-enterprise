Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our store

Our men's clothing collection comes in standard sizes L, XL, XXL, and XXXL. For items with color variations, you can pick your preferred color directly when adding to cart.

We deliver across Ghana. Same-day or 24h delivery within Kumasi via local dispatch riders, and 1–2 business days to Accra and other regions via VIP/STC bus station parcel dispatch. Delivery fee is shown transparently at checkout before you pay.

We accept Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money) and bank debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) securely processed through Paystack.

Yes! You can choose Free Store Pickup at checkout and collect your package directly at our physical shop at Kejetia Market, Kumasi (Mon–Sat 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Sunday closed). Our enterprise is verified on Google Business.

If an item doesn't fit, you can request an exchange within 3 days of delivery for unworn items with tags attached. Approved refunds are credited through Paystack back to your MoMo or card. Read our Refund & Delivery Policy.
