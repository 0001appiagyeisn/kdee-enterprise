    Set the shop's exact spot: search your area, then tap the map right on the shop. (A laptop usually can't find its exact location, so tapping the map is more reliable than "my location".)
    
          
    
            
            
          
    
          
      
          
      
          
      
            
            Check on Google Maps
          
      

          
      Delivery price
      
          
      Fee = base + (km × price per km) + (minutes × price per minute). Never below the minimum, then rounded up. Long trips use a cheaper per-km rate after the long-distance point.
      
          
      
            
      
            
      
          
      
          
      
            
      
            
      
          
      
          
      
            
      
            
      
          
      
          
      
            
      
            
      
          
      
          
      
            
      
            
      
          
      
          
      The two traffic factors only apply when live Google traffic is not set up (1.15 = 15% slower than an empty road; rush hours are about 6:30-9:30am and 4-7:30pm, Mon-Sat). With Google traffic, the real predicted time is used instead.
      

          
       Intercity Corridors & Road Condition Settings (Admin Internal)
      
          
      Set fair rates for different road qualities across Ghana (Accra VIP highway, Wassa rough roads, and other regions) so prices are accurate without showing extra surcharges to customers.
