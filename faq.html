Payments & Security

We accept all major Ghanaian payment methods processed safely through Paystack:

  • Mobile Money: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money. (Enter your number at checkout and confirm the prompt on your phone).
  • Bank Cards: Visa and Mastercard debit or credit cards.

While online payments are in test/offline periods, you can also send your order directly to our WhatsApp with one click to arrange payment.

Yes, 100% safe. All transactions are securely routed through Paystack, an internationally certified and regulated payment processor. We never see or store your Mobile Money PIN or card details on our servers.

If you don't receive an instant prompt on your phone: for MTN users, simply dial *170#, select Option 6 (My Wallet), and then select Option 3 (My Approvals) to enter your PIN and approve the pending transaction.

Delivery, Shipping & Pickup

We deliver nationwide across Ghana! When you choose "Deliver to me" at checkout, you can pin your exact location on our map or enter your address. The delivery fee is calculated and shown transparently before you pay, based on distance from our shop at Kejetia Market. For long-distance intercity deliveries outside Kumasi (e.g. Accra, Takoradi, Sunyani), parcels are conveniently sent via VIP or STC bus station parcel services.

  • Kumasi and nearby areas: Same-day delivery or within 24 hours via local dispatch riders.
  • Other Regions in Ghana (Accra, Takoradi, Cape Coast, Sunyani, Tamale, etc.): 1 to 2 business days via intercity bus parcel services.
  • Shop Pickup: Available immediately once your order status updates to "Ready for Pickup".

After placing an order, you will receive an SMS and email with your order confirmation. You can log into your account anytime on this website and click My Orders to watch real-time progress as your clothes are prepared, packaged, and dispatched.
